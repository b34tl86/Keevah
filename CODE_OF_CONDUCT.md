# Code of conduct

This repository is where people who use Keevah, or are thinking about it, ask questions, report bugs and share ideas. Help keep it a place where anyone can do that comfortably.

## Please

- Be courteous and patient, including when something isn't working.
- Assume good intent. Criticise the product, not the person.
- Stay on the subject of the issue or discussion.

## Please don't

- Harass, insult, threaten or demean anyone, or attack anyone for who they are.
- Post sexual, violent or hateful material.
- Share anyone's private information: no customer data, phone numbers, call recordings or transcripts, and nothing about another person that they haven't made public themselves.
- Spam, or advertise.

## If something's wrong

Use GitHub's **Report content** option on the post, or get in touch with TechTrust at [techtrust.ae](https://techtrust.ae). We treat reports in confidence.

## What we do about it

TechTrust looks after this repository and decides what breaks these rules. We may edit or remove posts, lock conversations, or block people from the repository, as seems fair.

This code applies everywhere in this repository: issues, discussions, and comments on releases.
