| You want to… | Go to |
|---|---|
| Ask how to do something, or how Keevah works | [Discussions → Q&A](https://github.com/b34tl86/Keevah/discussions/categories/q-a) |

## Before you ask

The answer may already be here:

- [Getting started](docs/getting-started.md), for downloading, installing and signing in;
- the [FAQ](README.md#faq) in the README;
- the [changelog](CHANGELOG.md), for what changed in each release;
- the [roadmap](ROADMAP.md), for what's planned.

## Keep customer data out

Discussions and issues are public. Never post callers' names or numbers, transcripts, call recordings, email addresses, passwords or API keys. Use made-up examples, and blank out screenshots before you share them.

## What to expect

Help in Discussions and Issues comes from the TechTrust team when we can give it, with no set response time. For a support agreement, talk to [TechTrust](https://techtrust.ae).
